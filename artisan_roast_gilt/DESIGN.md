## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an intimate, opulent cafe atmosphere rooted in artisan craft, deep roasted espresso, and classical architectural motifs. Designed for high-end cafe patrons, connoisseurs of single-origin roasts, and seekers of refined third-place hospitality, the visual language merges the brooding depth of a dimly lit, smoked-timber espresso salon with luminous gilded accents.

The aesthetic fuses Classical Luxury and Modern Tactile Minimalism:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Rich, non-distracting charcoal espresso and burnt-timber dark planes set a contemplative, sensory-rich tone.
- **Opulent Gilded Accents:** Rich honey golds and polished amber illuminate call-to-actions, status badges, and fine filigree details without overpowering content.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Subtle stylized fretwork and Greek-key border flourishes frame imagery and key containers, imparting an heirloom, museum-grade character.
- **Sensory Balance:** Decadent serif headings bring classic European editorial prestige, while crisp, unembellished geometric sans-serif body copy guarantees modern ordering fluidity.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the sensory environment of slow-extracted espresso, dark walnut cabinetry, and polished brass appointments under warm pendant illumination.

### Roles & Ratios
- **Base Surfaces (`#14100c`):** Deep roasted charcoal-espresso canvas providing zero-glare, velvety contrast.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#1e1814`, `#2a221c`):** Layered wood-smoke and raw cocoa containers. Card faces, drawers, and modal sheets sit in these elevated dark tiers.
- **Primary Gold (`#d4a34b`):** The signature artisan gold for hero buttons, active indicators, interactive icons, and key metadata.
- **Luminous Accent (`#e5b95e`):** Radiant champagne-gold utilized for hover states, focused rings, and delicate outer atmospheric glow blurs.
- **Burnished Bronze (`#8c6827`):** Muted metallic tone dedicated to subtle perimeter strokes, micro-borders, Greek-key flourishes, and secondary active rings.
- **Cream & Alabaster Text (`#f5ede0`, `#d6cbbe`, `#9e9185`):** Text eschews stark digital whites in favor of steamed-milk cream and linen hues, maintaining softness and legible elegance across dark backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes a deliberate balance between old-world editorial refinement and high-legibility transactional interaction.

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for menu category headers, hero storytelling, beverage titles, and narrative accents. Generous line heights allow dramatic serif serifs and italicized flourish variations to breathe without crowding.
- **Interface & Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Applied to ingredients, dietary notes, item options, pricing, and all input and transactional elements. Its warm geometry harmonizes naturally with circular and pill-shaped UI components.
- **Labeling & Micro-copy:** Labels leverage tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.04em - 0.08em`) and semi-bold/bold weights to maintain legibility at glanceable sizes on mobile devices during on-the-go ordering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an architectural grid model that privileges generous breathing room and framed culinary presentations.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12-column fluid grid capped at `1280px` maximum width. Canvas margins expand to `3rem` (`margin-desktop`) with `2rem` gutters (`gutter-lg`) to preserve negative space reminiscent of luxury tasting menus.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Menu cards reflow into 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` safe margins (`margin-mobile`) and `1rem` gutters (`gutter-sm`). Ordering interfaces transition into full-width cards with edge-aligned touch targets.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component padding adheres to a strict 4px/8px incremental cadence. Compact widgets, pill badges, and input states use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while luxury food and beverage cards utilize `space-lg` to `space-xl` for interior containment.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through dark-roast tonal stratification, gossamer bronze borders, and diffused amber incandescence rather than traditional grayscale drop shadows.

### Tonal Tiers
1. **Canvas Foundation (`#14100c`):** Deep charcoal ground level.
2. **Resting Containers (`#1e1814`):** Cards, lists, and drawers raised above canvas via a 1px border of `rgba(140, 104, 39, 0.25)`.
3. **Floating & Modal Elements (`#2a221c`):** Popovers, cart drawers, and sticky header bars featuring backdrop blur (`12px` to `16px`) and `rgba(212, 163, 75, 0.35)` border highlights.

### Amber Glow & Lighting
Interactive states deploy ambient backlighting:
- **Resting:** Fine 1px stroke of burnished bronze with an inset hairline highlight on upper edges.
- **Hover & Active:** An outer ambient halo tint using `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 163, 75, 0.18), 0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`. This mimics warm golden backlighting from cafe sconces.
- **Greek-Key Framing:** Key decorative vignettes feature hairline decorative dividers stamped with Greek-key corner anchors rendered in `rgba(212, 163, 75, 0.3)` line weight.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined contrast between crisp architectural framing and soft, tactile interaction targets:

- **Pill-Shaped Interactive Components:** All primary action buttons, status chips, pricing tags, and selection toggles utilize full capsule geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`), imparting an approachable, ergonomic feel.
- **Card Containers & Panels:** Menu and sensory cards feature subtle rounded corners (`16px` to `24px`) balanced by razor-sharp 1px golden inner strokes.
- **Decorative Flourishes:** Greek-key fret patterns and geometric corner accents introduce structured, classical rectilinearity, juxtaposing the soft curvature of cups, plates, and pill buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill):** Solid gold fill (`#d4a34b` gradient into `#e5b95e`), dark charcoal text (`#14100c`), bold `label-lg`. Hover transitions evoke an amber luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(229, 185, 94, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Pill Outline):** Transparent background, 1.5px border of `#d4a34b`, cream typography (`#f5ede0`). Hover fills with `rgba(212, 163, 75, 0.12)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Unbordered, gold text with an animated 1px gold underline on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped, compact containers (`height: 28px`, padding `0 12px`). Background in `#2a221c`, border in `rgba(212, 163, 75, 0.3)`, text in `#e5b95e` (`label-sm`).
- **Dietary & Tasting Notes (Single Origin, Oat Milk, Vegan):** Low-contrast espresso pills with subtle dot glyphs in polished gold.

### Menu & Product Cards
- **Construction:** Grounded in `#1e1814` with a 1px perimeter border in `#8c6827` (30% opacity).
- **Header & Imagery:** High-resolution product showcase accompanied by a subtle Greek-key brass watermark or corner fret ornament.
- **Typography:** Product title in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`), price in radiant gold (`title-md`), tasting notes in muted cream (`body-sm`).
- **Interaction:** Smooth scale (`1.015`) on hover with card border shifting from burnished bronze to luminous gold (`#d4a34b`).

### Input Fields
- **Form Fields:** Dark espresso cavity (`#14100c`), inset hairline border in `rgba(140, 104, 39, 0.35)`, roundedness at `12px` or pill-format for search bars. Text is typed in `#f5ede0` with warm cream placeholder text (`#9e9185`).
- **Focus State:** 1.5px gold outline (`#e5b95e`) accompanied by a subtle 4px amber focus halo.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`4px` radius) with a burnished bronze border; checked state presents an illuminated `#d4a34b` surface with an espresso checkmark.
- **Radios:** Concentric golden rings. Active state reveals a solid gold core enclosed within an amber glow boundary.

### Lists & Dividers
- **Menu Lists:** Clean rows separated by hairline gradient dividers (`transparent` → `rgba(212, 163, 75, 0.25)` → `transparent`).
- **Section Headers:** Accompanied by centered or flanking Greek-key linear motifs etched in gold foil styling.
